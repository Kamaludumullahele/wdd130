White Water Rafting

Our Mission

Our mission is to share the spirit of Banff’s wild rivers by delivering safe, unforgettable rafting adventures that connect families, friends, and explorers to the beauty, power, and wonder of the Canadian Rockies..

History

Founded in 2012, White Water Rafting began as a small group of river guides who shared one passion — showcasing the wild beauty of Banff and the Kicking Horse River. What started with a single raft and a handful of adventurous locals quickly grew as visitors from around the world discovered the thrill of whitewater in the Canadian Rockies.

Over the years, our team expanded, our gear evolved, and our trips became more diverse — but our mission stayed the same: to deliver safe, unforgettable river experiences for every kind of explorer. Today, we’re proud to be one of Banff’s trusted rafting outfitters, known for our friendly guides, family‑focused adventures, and deep respect for the rivers we call home.
